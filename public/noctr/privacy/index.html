Noctr – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · App: Noctr for Windows

In short: Noctr collects no personal data and sends nothing anywhere.

What Noctr does

Noctr is a Windows app that tints the screen red for comfortable night work and shows optional eye-care reminders. Everything it does happens on your PC.

Data stored on your PC

Noctr saves its settings (for example the chosen tint level, brightness, reminder intervals and your chosen location) and a small technical log in your Windows user profile (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Noctr). These files never leave your PC. Uninstalling Noctr or deleting that folder removes them.

Location

Auto mode uses a location to calculate sunrise and sunset. You can pick a city from a built-in list, type coordinates, or allow Windows location once. The location is used only on your PC for this calculation and is stored only in the settings file. Noctr never uploads it.

Network, analytics and advertising

Noctr does not connect to the internet, contains no analytics or tracking, shows no ads and does not share data with anyone. The only link in the app (“Website” in About) opens your browser when you click it.

System changes

If you choose “Unlock extreme levels”, Noctr (with your administrator approval) sets one Windows display setting (GdiIcmGammaRange) that allows stronger colour changes. It contains no personal data.

Children

Noctr does not collect data from anyone, including children.
